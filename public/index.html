How it works

Three simple steps. Available in English, हिंदी, मराठी, தமிழ் and తెలుగు.

1

Login with your mobile

We send a one-time code by SMS. No password to remember.

2

Tell us your address

House or shop number, area, city and pincode. We find it on the map for you.

3

Take the photos

Stand at your entrance and follow the on-screen guide. Voice guidance is available.

🔒 Your privacy comes first

We only photograph your entrance and nameplate (or shop signboard) — never the inside of your home. Photos are used only to verify your address and are deleted automatically after 90 days.

Read the privacy notice

🤝 Prefer an agent?

If you can't do it yourself, you can request an agent visit at any step. Nobody is rejected because a photo didn't work — our team reviews it instead.

Questions

Do I need to be at the address?

Yes. Your phone's location is checked against the address you enter, so please be at the entrance when you take the photos.

What if a photo is blurry or too dark?

We check each photo instantly and tell you how to fix it — for example, hold the phone steady or turn on a light.

Can I stop and continue later?

Yes. Log in again and tap "Continue" on your verification. It stays open for 3 days.

Which phones work?

Any recent Android phone with Chrome, or iPhone with Safari. Make sure camera and location permissions are allowed for this site.
